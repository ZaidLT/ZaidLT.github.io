Request

A person starts with a need.

Voice and messaging connect a person's request to the assistant. I owned the integration layer across these entry points.

Read all five stages
  1. Request. Voice and messaging connect a person's request to the assistant. I owned the integration layer across these entry points.
  2. Memory. Structured facts, semantic retrieval, and time-dependent relationships provide context. I was the primary engineer on the separate PostgreSQL and pgvector memory service.
  3. Coordination. A coordinator and specialist agents connect context with tools. I co-led the Google ADK platform and deployed two MCP servers exposing existing platform tools.
  4. Approval. The repair workflow pauses before a consequential external action. I built approval controls, scoped inputs, duplicate-action prevention, and decision audit records.
  5. Action. After approval, the workflow can proceed and retain its state and outcome. Calling and repair workflows were among the seven capabilities I built that shipped to production.
