Document intelligence

Document intelligence at scale

I built and operated an eight-stage platform that prepared manufacturer manuals for AI search. A small model read the first three pages of screened manuals and classified the clear cases.

I reserved deeper reads and stronger models for uncertain cases, with a second model as the final cross-check. The pipeline checked brands and model numbers against the manual itself and flagged unresolved matches for review.
